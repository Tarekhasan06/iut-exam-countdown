# Reference background notes

Source: https://kopalethakle.vercel.app/

The reference uses a full-viewport aerial IUT campus photograph as the page background. The image is darkened with a charcoal/black overlay so the subject stays subdued and centered text remains legible. The composition is intentionally minimal: centered small uppercase eyebrow, centered serif university title, a short italic line, and large countdown values arranged in translucent dark rounded cards. The page has no visible side panels or bright color accents; its mood is cinematic, nostalgic, and graduation-focused.

Adaptation decision: bring the reference’s full-bleed campus-photo + dark overlay treatment into the exam countdown app background, while preserving the existing editorial countdown card and schedule rail as the functional foreground. Use the campus image as a low-contrast fixed/cover background layer for the entire page, with an additional warm ivory veil behind the app content to preserve readability and keep the existing dashboard hierarchy intact.
